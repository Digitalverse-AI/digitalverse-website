Constraint-first

Where to start.

We do not start with a list of tasks to automate. We start by finding the constraint: the process, decision point or handover that is limiting the whole business. Improving anything else only helps in proportion to how much it actually holds the business back, so the bottleneck comes first. That is the approach set out in AI agents should target bottlenecks, not tasks.

For many growing small businesses the front office, meaning timely response to leads and enquiries, follow-up, bookings, and handovers, is the place to start. It is usually the fastest place to prove that managed intelligence can make the business more responsive without losing control. Prove the value there, measure what changed, then move to the next constraint. Our customer story shows the same discipline applied to re-engaging existing customers during a quiet trading period.
